## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes **Telecom Customer Churn** data using **Microsoft Excel** to uncover why customers leave and what patterns predict churn. An interactive dashboard was built to help telecom business teams identify at-risk customers and take proactive retention actions.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Customer churn is one of the biggest challenges for telecom companies. Losing a customer costs far more than retaining one. This project uses historical customer data to:
- Identify **who is churning** and **why**
- Discover the **key drivers of churn**
- Provide insights that help reduce churn rate

## 💡 Key Insights

- Customers on **month-to-month contracts** churn at **3x the rate** of annual contract holders
- **High monthly charges (>₹65/month)** are strongly correlated with churn
- **Senior citizens** have a significantly higher churn rate compared to younger customers
- Customers **without tech support or online security** services churn more frequently
- **Fiber optic internet users** show higher churn than DSL users

---

## 📈 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis:
1. **Offer discounts** to convert month-to-month customers to annual contracts
2. **Target senior citizens** with simplified plans and dedicated support
3. **Bundle tech support** services to improve retention
4. **Review fiber optic pricing** as high costs may be driving churn
